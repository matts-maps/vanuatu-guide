Vanuatu: Data sources

Back to the guide

The guide is drawn by hand, but the places, roads, coastlines and forecasts behind it come from the open sources below. Thank you to everyone who collects and shares them.

Map data

  • Roads, rivers, villages, houses, hills, volcano heights, hotels and guesthouses, kava bars, museums, wharves and landings: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. Extracted by Geofabrik on 22 November 2026. Hills and volcano heights use the elevation recorded in OpenStreetMap, and hills under 150 metres are left off.
  • Coastlines and island outlines: Natural Earth (public domain) and the Global Self-consistent, Hierarchical, High-resolution Geography database.

Airports, ports and routes

  • Airport positions and codes: OurAirports (public domain).
  • Volcano status: active, dormant and extinct volcanoes follow the Volcanoes of the World catalogue of the Smithsonian Institution Global Volcanism Program (volcano.si.edu), which gives each volcano's last known eruption. Active means erupted in 2000 or later, dormant means erupted in the last 11,700 years but not since 2000, and extinct means no known eruption in that time. Status can change, so check the official Vanuatu Geohazards Observatory alert level before a visit.
  • Port Vila main port position: the World Port Index, published by the United States National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency. Other wharves, landings and marinas come from OpenStreetMap.
  • Flight and ferry routes: drawn from routes listed by Air Vanuatu, the ferry operators and the Vanuatu Tourism Office. Lines are approximate and ports and end points are indicative. Schedules change often, so check before you travel.

Weather

  • Weather: forecasts from Open-Meteo.com (licence CC BY 4.0), refreshed about hourly.
  • The forecast bar shows Port Vila, Santo or Tanna, whichever is nearest the island you are looking at.

Places and descriptions

  • Place descriptions are summarised from the Vanuatu Tourism Office, Lonely Planet and information from Air Vanuatu and the ferry operators. They are paraphrased, not copied.
  • Annual event dates come from the Vanuatu Tourism Office events calendar. They move from year to year, so check before you book.
  • The position of Mount Tabwemasana was checked against Wikipedia coordinates. The Ramada Resort by Wyndham Port Vila was added from a hotel listing because it is missing from OpenStreetMap.
  • Places marked approximate on their cards have a pin that sits near, not exactly on, the real spot.
  • The guide's text was compiled in November 2026. Opening hours, fees, flight and ferry timetables and event dates were not checked live, so confirm them before you travel.
  • Distances and travel times from Port Vila are estimates. Opening hours, fees, tides and schedules change, so confirm locally.

Your location
