Getting there

  • Direct flights reach Port Vila in about 3 hours from Sydney or Brisbane and about 3.5 hours from Auckland.
  • Domestic flights from Port Vila reach Tanna, Espiritu Santo (Luganville), Malekula, Ambrym, Pentecost, Epi and the northern islands. Schedules change often and bad weather can delay flights, so build in spare days.

Getting around

  • On Efate, hire a car or quad bike in Port Vila and drive the ring road, or join a small-group island tour. On the outer islands, guesthouses usually arrange guides, trucks and boats.
  • Few shops along the loop: pack fruit and water.
  • Village attractions charge small entry fees in cash vatu. Fees change, so ask locally.

Between the islands

  • Flights: Air Vanuatu connects Port Vila to Santo and Tanna with its larger planes, and smaller aircraft serve other islands such as Malekula, Ambrym, Ambae and Epi. Dashed blue lines on the map show routes listed by the airline and tourism office. Schedules change, so check before you plan.
  • Ferries: Vanuatu Ferry and Big Sista sail Port Vila to Malekula to Luganville, about 24 hours end to end. The MV Tauraken sails Port Vila to Erromango to Tanna, sometimes on to Aneityum. The MV Brisk and MV Tina serve Ambrym. Dashed green lines show these, drawn around the islands. Ports and end points are approximate.

When to go

  • The drier months, roughly May to October, are most comfortable. The wetter cyclone season runs roughly November to April, so check forecasts and stay flexible.
  • Cruise ships crowd Blue Lagoon, Mele Cascades, Champagne Beach and Mystery Island. Go early or late. Land diving on Pentecost runs on Saturdays from about April to June.

Cyclone season

  • Tropical cyclones can hit Vanuatu from about November to April, most often from January to March. Flights and ferries can be cancelled at short notice, so leave spare days and take travel insurance.
  • Follow official warnings from the Vanuatu Meteorology and Geo-hazards Department and ask your accommodation about local advice. Volcano alert levels for Tanna, Ambrym and Ambae are published by the same department.
